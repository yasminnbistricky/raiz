What we do

We plan routes, work out timing, and arrange transport, guides, rooms and activities with local suppliers on your behalf. We handle the conversations in Portuguese and we answer your messages while you travel.

What you pay us is for that work. It is our only revenue from your trip.

What we are not

We are not the company that drives you, guides you, houses you, feeds you or teaches you to kitesurf. Those services are provided by independent local businesses, and the contract for each of them is between you and them.

You pay those suppliers directly, in local currency, at their own published prices. We do not mark them up and we receive no commission from them, which is why we have no reason to steer you to one supplier over another.

When you ask us to arrange flights or accommodation, that booking is made by a partner travel agency under its own terms, and the commission is theirs.

We choose suppliers we know and we stop working with any that treat travellers, particularly women travelling alone, without respect. But we do not control them, and we are not liable for how they perform their own service.

Prices and payment

Our fees are shown on the site in euros, with the equivalent in pounds and dollars. The amount charged is the euro amount. Your bank may apply its own exchange rate and fees.

Costs paid on the ground to local suppliers are shown in Brazilian reais, because that is the currency you will pay them in. Those amounts are theirs, not ours, and can change.

Payment is made through our payment provider before the work begins.

Cancellation and refunds

Video call: you can move or cancel it free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment. Inside 24 hours, or if you do not show up, the fee is not refunded.

Trip planning: half of the fee is due when you book and is not refundable, because that is when the work starts. The other half is refundable in full until we send you the first draft of your plan. After we send it, the fee is not refundable, because the work has been delivered.

If we cancel: you get everything back, including the non-refundable half.

Cancelling a supplier, once your trip is arranged, follows that supplier's own rules, which we will tell you before anything is booked.

What we need from you

Accurate information. A plan built on the wrong dates or the wrong number of people is not a plan.

Valid documents, and any visa you need. We can tell you what we know, but entry to Brazil is the decision of the Brazilian authorities and it is your responsibility.

Travel insurance. We do not sell it and we do not require it, but the places we send you to are remote, and we strongly recommend it.

Honesty about health, fitness and swimming ability, so we do not put you somewhere that is wrong for you.

Things nobody controls

Rain, drought, tide, wind, sand, road conditions, river levels and the opening hours of a national park are not ours to command. The lagoons are higher in some months than others and can be low in a dry year.

When something changes, we rearrange what we can, as fast as we can. What we cannot do is refund a supplier's service that you received, or promise weather.

If something goes wrong

Tell us while you are still there. Almost everything is fixable in the moment and almost nothing is fixable a month later.

Our responsibility is limited to the fee you paid us, except where the law does not allow that limit. Nothing here removes rights you have as a consumer under the law where you live.
