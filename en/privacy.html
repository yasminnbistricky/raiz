Privacy Policy

Plain language, because you should be able to read this without a lawyer. If anything here is unclear, write to us and we will explain it.

Last updated: 30 August 2026

Who we are

Raiz plans trips to remote parts of Brazil and arranges the ground logistics. We are the controller of the personal data described here.

Legal entity: [RAZAO SOCIAL A DEFINIR]. Company registration: [CNPJ A DEFINIR]. Registered address: [ENDERECO A DEFINIR].

For anything about your data, write to yasminnbistricky@gmail.com. We answer in English, Portuguese, French and Spanish.

What we collect

When you contact us: your name, your email address, your phone or WhatsApp number, and whatever you choose to tell us about your trip, such as your dates, who is travelling with you and what you want to see.

When you ask for a free guide: your email address.

When you become a client: the above, plus what we need to arrange your trip, which may include flight times, accommodation preferences and dietary or accessibility needs.

When you visit the site: our hosting provider records standard server logs, including IP address and browser type. We do not use these to identify you.

We do not ask for and do not want your passport number, your card number or your identity document. If you send one to us anyway, we will delete it.

Why we use it, and on what legal basis

To answer your message and prepare a plan for you. Legal basis: performance of a contract with you, or steps taken at your request before entering one.

To send you a guide or an email you asked for. Legal basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time using the link in any email we send.

To keep records of bookings and payments. Legal basis: our legal and tax obligations in Brazil.

To measure whether our advertising works. Legal basis: your consent, given through the cookie banner.

Who we share it with

Local suppliers, and only what they need. A driver gets your name and your pick up time. A guesthouse gets your name and your dates. Nobody gets your whole file.

A partner travel agency based in the Lençóis Maranhenses, when you ask us to arrange flights or accommodation. That agency is paid by commission from the supplier, not by us, and it handles those bookings under its own terms.

Our email and scheduling tools, which store your address so we can write to you and hold your appointment.

Meta Platforms, which delivers and measures our advertising. If you reach us through an ad or a lead form on Facebook or Instagram, Meta processes that data under its own policy as well as ours.

Payment providers, which process your payment. We never see or store your full card number.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Cookies and tracking

The site itself needs no cookies to work, and sets none for analytics or advertising unless you accept them in the banner.

If you accept, we may use the Meta Pixel to understand which ads bring people here. You can refuse, change your mind later through the banner, and separately control ad personalisation in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

Your browser also lets you block or delete cookies for any site, including this one.

Sending your data outside your country

Raiz is based in Brazil and your trip happens in Brazil, so your data is processed here and shared with suppliers here.

If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, this means your data leaves that area. We rely on the transfer being necessary to perform the contract you asked for, and we share only what each supplier needs to do their part.

Some of the tools we use are based in the United States and store data there.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, ask us to send it to you in a portable format, object to a particular use, or withdraw a consent you gave.

Write to yasminnbistricky@gmail.com and we will answer within 30 days. We do not charge for this.

If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live. In Brazil that is the ANPD. In the European Union it is the authority of your member state.

How long we keep it

If you write to us and do not become a client, we keep the conversation for two years, in case you come back, and then delete it.

If you become a client, we keep the trip file for five years after your trip, and the records that Brazilian tax law requires us to keep for as long as it requires.

If you subscribe to emails, we keep your address until you unsubscribe.

How we protect it

Your data lives in accounts protected by strong passwords and two factor authentication, and only the two people who run Raiz have access to it.

No system is perfect. If a breach ever puts your rights at risk, we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

Children

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect their data. When children travel with their family, we handle their details only as part of the family booking and only with the parent's instruction.

Changes to this policy

If we change something that matters, we will change the date at the top of this page and, when the change is significant, tell the people we have an email address for.
